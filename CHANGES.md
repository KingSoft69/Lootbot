# Changes

Newest first. Each entry: what changed, the evidence for it, and what result would show it worked.

## 0.1.0

First version. Loots containers at targets from the learner (chests, trapped chests, barrels,
placed shulker boxes broken and picked up whole), shulker boxes first then the most valuable items,
unloads at home, reports every target visit. The learner tunes ten settings (`learner/space.json`)
and runs new releases on a canary bot before everyone else.
